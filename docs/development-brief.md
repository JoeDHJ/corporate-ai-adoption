# Corporate AI Adoption — Development Brief

Date: 2026-11-16
Repository: JoeDHJ/corporate-ai-adoption
Product: Corporate AI Adoption and Workforce Strategy.

## Decision to support

A management team wants to understand its AI adoption evidence, changing skill and hiring needs, and the relative cost of training, internal mobility and external hiring. Build a complete public-data consulting case and a reusable analytical product that answer those questions.

## Scope

- Establish a reproducible Python analytical package with a usable CLI, testable modules, a source registry, schemas and a local interactive dashboard. Keep installation and operation practical on Windows.
- Acquire a bounded, substantive corpus of official public company filings for a defensible peer group and multiple periods. Select coverage based on availability and employer relevance; target roughly 10–20 companies where feasible. Store source references and excerpts only where redistribution permits.
- Measure adoption with evidence spans and a reviewable taxonomy: actual deployment, pilots, planned investment, AI hiring/innovation and generic promotional statements. Distinguish the measured dimensions; a keyword hit alone cannot establish deployment.
- Compare an interpretable baseline with any NLP/LLM classifier on a documented, actually reviewed sample. Publish evaluation counts, error examples and limitations of the actual annotation process; do not invent human labels or independent validation.
- Analyze firm/industry hiring and occupational/skill composition using genuine permitted posting or official employment data when available. Keep company disclosures, vacancy observations and modeled staffing inputs separate. Do not infer company workforce stocks or causal adoption effects from posting counts alone.
- Build a workforce strategy model comparing training, redeployment and recruiting under explicit skill requirements, costs, constraints and adoption scenarios. Include transparent cash-flow/NPV/payback calculations where inputs support them, with sensitivity to the assumptions that change the decision.
- Complete at least one substantive public-company adoption case and one linked workforce strategy case. Label the management engagement and internal staffing/cost inputs as a consulting scenario where they are fictional.
- Produce an executive research report, short executive brief, management presentation outline and exhibits, dashboard and 90/180/365-day implementation roadmap. Use Markdown and reusable figures/data by default; no unrequested Word/PDF generation.
- Lead the README with the business question, inspectable findings, one visual, methods and reproduction commands. Provide a two-minute demonstration script and complete, readable module screenshots.
- Document portfolio.v1 public outputs for optional Atlas/Career Fit consumption. Keep this project runnable without sibling services.

## Completion evidence

Use 2–3 actual official employer JDs for corporate/workforce economist, AI strategy or economic consulting roles to test professional fit. Verify filing parsing and citations, adoption classification edge cases, denominators, time/currency units, scenario arithmetic, empty/malformed inputs, clean install and browser workflows. Have independent read-only reviewers examine requirements, logic, edges, code quality, tests and actual runs; fix material findings and re-check affected paths before publishing.

## Development workflow

Develop in scoped feature branches, validate the public-data case, obtain independent review and resolve material findings before release. Public artifacts contain permitted public observations and clearly labeled scenario inputs. Preserve source references for every published result and keep private or unpublished research data outside the repository.
