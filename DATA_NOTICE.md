# Data access and rights

The MIT license applies to original software and authored analytical text. It does not transfer ownership of third-party issuer reports, excerpts or employer pages.

Company annual reports and SEC-filed issuer content remain issuer copyright. Only bounded short evidence excerpts are included for source-checkable analysis (at most 200 whitespace-delimited words per report across the selected three spans). Full downloaded reports and full parsed text remain in the local ignored data/raw directory. A SEC-hosted copy is not treated as US government authorship of issuer content. Source URLs, report fiscal labels, retrieval dates, byte sizes and locators accompany the excerpts.

BLS aggregate facts are transcribed from the official table and attributed in each record. They are US federal statistical data. The selected job pages are represented by links, native IDs and paraphrased requirements; full job-page text is not redistributed.

All source access was public, without login, restricted datasets or paid-API dependence. Some initial issuer/CDN URLs failed or returned incomplete content; the final registry contains the successfully accessed official issuer/SEC alternatives. No access-control bypass or certificate validation disabling was used. Failed attempts remain in local working records; published source records refer to successful inputs.

Staffing, cost, benefit, success-rate and capability-demand values are fictional consulting scenario inputs and are marked scenario. No unpublished research data, individual/household records, resumes, applicant data, private company records or real client engagement was used.
