Economic reasoning behind the model

Task substitution releases time; source checking and exception handling create complementary tasks. Productive capacity becomes cash or contribution only when a budget or demand mechanism realizes it. Backfill and training time are opportunity costs. Integration and learning delay benefits, creating an investment ramp.
